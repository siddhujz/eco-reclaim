Eco-Reclaim Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-28 · Notice version 3

Developer: Siddhartha Kodali

Contact for privacy questions and requests: ecoreclaim.support@gmail.com

What the game keeps, and where

Eco-Reclaim has no account and no server of its own. Everything it needs is saved on your device only: your progress (zones, stars, Bio, upgrades, belt items), your settings (sound, music, vibration, aim, reminders), the year you were born and your ad choice. We, the developer, do not receive any of it. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), that backup may include this save, under your account with them; this is what lets your progress move to a new phone.

Reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.

Your age

On first launch the game asks for your year of birth. It is used only to decide whether ad services may run (see below) and stays on your device. If you are under 18, or have not told us your age, no advertising service is started at all; rewards that adults can earn by watching a video are simply free for you.

Purchases

Optional purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment under their own terms and privacy policies. The game only learns which item you bought, so it can deliver it. We never see your payment details. The store's purchase system built into the game (Google Play's billing library on Android, StoreKit on iPhone) also sends Google or Apple its own technical diagnostics, for every player, including players under 18; their privacy policies cover that data. Purchases are never needed to play or to win.

Ads (players aged 18 and over only)

The game never shows ads you did not ask for. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a reward (for example a revive). These rewarded videos are provided by Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (such as Unity Ads). To show and measure an ad, and to prevent fraud, they can receive your IP address (from which a rough location can be inferred), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), identifiers for this app and device (such as an app-set ID), how you interact with the ad, and whether you have bought something in the game (purchase history). Only if you choose personalised ads do they also receive your device's advertising ID. Their own privacy policies apply.

On Android you choose personalised or not personalised ads the first time ads could run and can change it any time in Settings · Privacy, where you can also turn on "Do not sell or share". On iPhone ads are never personalised: the game does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across apps and never shows the tracking prompt. If your phone's region is set to Brazil, ads are always non-personalised, whatever you choose.

The game engine

The game is built with Unity. The engine's own usage and hardware statistics are switched off in the build where the engine allows it; the game has no analytics of its own.

Your rights and choices

In Settings · Privacy you can:

  • change your ad choice;
  • turn on "Do not sell or share";
  • correct your age;
  • read this notice;
  • delete all of your data from the device.

Your phone's own settings let you reset or limit the advertising ID. For anything else (a question, a complaint, or a request about data an ad network may hold) write to ecoreclaim.support@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Children

Eco-Reclaim is a general-audience game. Players under 18 get no ad services, and the game itself sends nothing about them anywhere; the only traffic is the store's own purchase system described under Purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the game asks again on the next start. Last updated: 2026-09-28.
